Data source: Yahoo Finance via yfinance, hourly bars from 2026-08-10 09:30 to 2026-11-02 15:30 (420 candles)
BH start price: ~137.11, end price: ~68.16, BH return: approx -50.3% full period (or -44.4% post-indicator warmup)
